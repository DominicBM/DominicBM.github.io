Dominic Byrd-McDevitt

I am a cultural professional specializing in Wikipedia, digital engagement, open data, and open government. I am Director of Community Engagement at the Digital Public Library of America. Previously, I worked at the National Archives and Records Administration, leading their Wikipedia initiative and other citizen archivist programs.
